01 / THE IDEA

A sandbox that belongs
to the players.

I started Pixelia because I enjoy games where players aren't just following a fixed story. They build, collect, trade and turn an empty world into something personal.

THE GOAL

Easy to start. Hard to put down.

I want to make a game that feels simple when you first enter, but gives players enough freedom and progression to keep building and exploring.

01

Players shape their own worlds instead of being limited to one path.
